---

## Architecture Notes

Recommended schema layers:

- `ConsultarRegistrosResponseSchema`
- `ProjetoV2RecordSchema`
- `MensagemRetornoSchema`
- `FilhoDatasetSchema`
- child schemas for:
  - tempos permanencia
  - requisitos
  - orcamentos
  - horas lancadas
  - historicos
  - entregas
  - cronogramas
  - aprovadores

Recommended strictness:

- strict on expected object shapes
- allow nullable fields where the real sample proves nullability
- do not coerce business values yet; mapping belongs to later stories
